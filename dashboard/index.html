Playbook

Your process, so every job is clear-cut from the first call.

📞 Before you quote

  • Get exact surfaces and areas in writing (avoid "can you also…" after the job)
  • Confirm an outdoor water source
  • Ask for photos if they can't meet in person (quote a range)
  • Note the town and add the travel fee
  • Ask about pools, plants, fabric furniture near the work area

🚩 Walk away / be careful

  • Chewed-up or soft wooden decks
  • Oxidation on siding
  • Broken or cracked siding
  • Old siding not painted in a long time
  • Anything where washing could cause more damage

🌤 Job-day conditions

  • Sunny is ideal; a light sprinkle is OK, no rain
  • Patio restoration: patio must dry fully before sanding (2–3 days)
  • Warn about dirt in the pool if working nearby

🧴 Bleach safety

  • Pre-rinse every surface before bleach
  • Keep away from wood, fabric, and windows
  • Rinse plants and windows thoroughly
  • Watch water drop-off points so bleach doesn't pool

🏠 House wash method

  • Foam cannon: apply bleach bottom → top (no streaks)
  • Pump sprayer for precise small areas
  • Rinse top → bottom, nearly parallel to siding

🧰 Gear

  • Honda GX200 pressure washer
  • Foam cannon + pump-up sprayer
  • Round surface cleaner (patios, large flat areas)
  • Turbo nozzle (fences); low pressure on wood
  • Polymeric sand (patio restoration)

Why it matters

  • Customers worry about age and that you're not an LLC. Insurance and an LLC answer both worries.
  • Insurance protects you if bleach or pressure damages property.
  • Real estate agents usually want an insured vendor, so insurance helps you land those contacts.
  • Selling points to lead with: personal service, a game plan before every job, patio restoration (most competitors don't offer it), fast replies.
